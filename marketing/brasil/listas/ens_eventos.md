## 1. ENS: what it is

- Brazil's insurance-sector school. Founded in 1971 in Rio de Janeiro (as Funenseg) and turned 55 on 30 Jun 2026.
- Maintained by **CNseg** (insurers' confederation) and **Fenacor** (brokers' federation). Sources: CNseg page https://cnseg.org.br/sobre-nos/o-mercado-segurador/escola-de-negocios-e-seguros-ens and CQCS 02-Jul-2026 (https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/ens-celebra-55-anos-de-protagonismo-no-mercado-de-seguros/).
- Website: https://www.ens.edu.br
- News blog: https://acontece.ens.edu.br
- Events page: https://www.ens.edu.br/eventos
- Central de Atendimento: 0800 025 3322. MBA enquiries: inscricao@ens.edu.br (Apr-2026 press). Both UNVERIFIED on ens.edu.br.

### What ENS offers brokers

1. **Curso para Habilitação de Corretores de Seguros (CHCS).** The main entry route into the profession.
   - Online and live, **9 months**, 3 modules: Capitalização; Vida e Previdência; Seguros de Danos.
   - Requirements: ensino médio completo and age 18+.
   - Graduates receive a certificate that lets them apply for **SUSEP registration without a further exam** (ENS's own statement as indexed).
   - A shorter "Seguros de Danos (Demais Ramos)" track (about 5 months) is available for those already qualified in Vida e Previdência.
   - Recent intakes: 2 or 9 March 2026 (2026 class), and 11 Aug 2025 (1,030 places).
   - **Next CHCS intake after Oct 2026: not published in what I could see.** It is probably March 2027, following the annual pattern, but that is UNVERIFIED.
   - Sources: https://acontece.ens.edu.br/aulas-para-formacao-de-corretores-iniciam-em-marco-de-2026/ ; Regulamento CHCS 2025.1: https://prdapi.ens.edu.br/media/course/visual-content/Regulamento_CHCS_Online_Todos_os_Ramos_2025.1_v.3.pdf
2. **Exame para Habilitação de Corretores de Seguros (EHCS).** The exam route, with no course required.
   - **84º EHCS** (last of 2026): online, **30 Nov to 3 Dec 2026**. Registration **until 11 Nov 2026** via ens.edu.br → "Ser Corretor".
   - Tests open 18h–19h (Brasília time), 2 hours each. Seven modalities (Capitalização, Vida e Previdência, Seguros de Danos, Todos os Ramos, etc.).
   - Fee from about R$555 (previous edition). 50% off for students of the preparatory course.
   - Example regulation, 82º EHCS 2026: https://prdapi.ens.edu.br/media/exam/REGULAMENTO_-_82o_EHCS_1aEd._2026_VR.AP.pdf
   - The 2027 exam calendar has not been published (UNVERIFIED). In 2026 there were at least editions in about Mar/Apr, Jul (20–23 Jul) and Nov/Dec.
3. **MBAs (pós-graduação, remote).**
   - **MBA Gestão Avançada de Seguros.** 9th class started 13 Apr 2026. 14 months, Monday and Wednesday evenings, 3 modules with CIMMO (Canada). Programme PDF: https://prdapi.ens.edu.br/media/course/visual-content/Programa_MBA_Gest%C3%A3o_Avan%C3%A7ada_de_Seguros_-_v9.1.CIMMO.pdf
   - **MBA Gestão Jurídica em Contratos de Seguro e Inovação.**
   - **MBA Finanças e Seguros.**
   - All three started in April 2026 (360 h, 14–18 months).
   - Early registration for the next Gestão Jurídica and Gestão Avançada classes closed 10 and 16 Sep 2026. **Start date of the next classes is not published**, likely around Q1/Q2 2027 (UNVERIFIED).
4. **Graduação (EaD).** Gestão de Seguros, plus Gestão Financeira (new in 2026: 2 years, 1,600 h). The August 2026 intake brought 180+ and 30+ freshmen respectively. Next intake not confirmed.
5. **Short courses and extension (mostly online and live).** Relevant to brokers in Q4 2026:
   - **Saúde Suplementar e Regras da ANS.** Starts 13 Oct 2026 (registration to 9 Oct), 7 weeks.
   - **Inteligência Artificial para Corretores de Seguros.** Starts 26 Oct 2026 (registration to 23 Oct), 18 h over 9 sessions.
   - **Seguros D&O e E&O – Novas Oportunidades de Negócios.** Starts 3 Nov 2026 (registration to 30 Oct), 18 h over 4 weeks.
   - **Seven free courses** (LGPD no setor de seguros, operação de corretoras, marketing digital, etc.), with registration open until the end of 2026. Source: https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/ens-oferece-sete-cursos-gratuitos-para-capacitacao-de-profissionais-do-mercado-de-seguros/
   - Certificação avançada em Seguros de Pessoas: dates not found.
   - Programa "Sou Corretor" (bundle of short sales courses; 2023 info, current status UNVERIFIED).
   - Source for the Oct/Nov courses: Revista Apólice Oct-2026, https://revistaapolice.com.br/2026/10/ens-promove-formacoes-sobre-ia-saude-e-seguros-de-rc/
6. **Events and immersions.**
   - **Fórum "Seguros, Sinistros e Desastres".** 10 Nov 2026, 08h30, Hotel Meliá Paulista, São Paulo (listed on ens.edu.br/eventos per search index).
   - **Imersão Internacional ENS na Itália (2nd class).** 25–29 Jan 2027 in Trieste (Generali visit), Florence and Rome. Registration until 20 Dec 2026. Source: https://cqcs.com.br/noticia/ens-abre-inscricoes-para-nova-imersao-internacional-na-italia-com-foco-no-mercado-europeu-de-seguros/
   - Sincors often negotiate member discounts at ENS (e.g. Sincor-SP, Sincor-ES), and ENS partners with Sincors on congress logistics.

**Why this matters for Parley Notes.** The CHCS and EHCS produce a steady flow of **new brokers**: about 3,100 new SUSEP registrations per quarter (CQCS, Sep 2026). New brokers set up their tooling from scratch. ENS short courses on AI for brokers (Oct 2026) show the target audience is already being educated on AI.
